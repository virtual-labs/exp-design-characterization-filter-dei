1. Click the "Concept" tab to read the basic theory of the ring resonator bandpass filter.
2. Click the "Formula" tab to note down the relevant design and resonance formulas.
3. Click the "Why 50 Ω?" tab to understand why 50 Ω is used as the standard reference impedance.
4. Click the "Design & Simulation" tab to open the design and simulation dashboard.
5. Enter the design parameters (W, h, εr, tanδ, f0, BW, filter order N, ripple) in Panel 1 (Design Parameters).
6. Observe the ring filter layout updating live in Panel 2 (Live Geometry View), and use the "DIMS" toggle to view dimension annotations.
7. Note the live-calculated values shown in Panel 3 (Live Design Values).
8. Click the "Run Simulation" button to generate the S-parameter response.
9. Observe the S-Parameters (Magnitude) graph showing |S21| and |S11| in Panel 4.
10. Observe the VSWR curve in Panel 5.
11. Observe the reflection coefficient locus on the Smith Chart in Panel 6.
12. Observe the equivalent circuit diagram and its computed values in Panel 7.
13. Select "Electric Field" or "Magnetic Field" mode and move the frequency slider to observe the Field Distribution in Panel 8.
14. Change one design parameter at a time and click "Run Simulation" again to observe its effect on the filter response.
15. Use the "Reset" button to restore the default design values if needed.
16. Record all observations and write a conclusion based on the results obtained.
